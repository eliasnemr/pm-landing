From “anyone free?” to match point in three taps

The serve

Post or join a match

Open matches near you, filtered by level, time, and side of the court you like to play. One tap reserves your spot.

The rally

Court booked, fee split

We hold the court at a partner club and split the price four ways automatically. No group-chat maths, no no-shows unpunished.

The point

Log the score, level up

Report the result in seconds. Your level adjusts after every set, so your next match is always the right one.

Level engine

A number that actually means something

Self-rated levels lie. Your PADELMATCH.XYZ level is earned on court — every set you play against every opponent refines it, from 0.5 (first racket) to 7.0 (tour-grade).

  • Separate reliability score — the more you play, the more it trusts you
